# Cobblemon model assets

Extracted from the user-provided Cobblemon-fabric-1.8.1+1.21.1.jar.
Only the geometry and textures for Pikachu, Eevee and Charmander are included, not the mod binary.
Source paths: assets/cobblemon/bedrock/pokemon/models/ and assets/cobblemon/textures/pokemon/.
Assets belong to their respective authors. Project: https://cobblemon.com
The website renders the original Bedrock cube geometry and texture atlases with Three.js.
